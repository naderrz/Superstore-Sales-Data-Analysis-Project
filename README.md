# Superstore Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of a Superstore sales dataset using Python. The main objective is to explore sales performance, identify business patterns, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate shipping performance through statistical analysis and data visualization.

The analysis covers multiple business dimensions, including sales trends, product categories, customer segments, geographical distribution, and shipping delays.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue distribution.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate product and sub-category performance.
- Identify top-performing customers and states.
- Compare sales across regions and customer segments.
- Analyze shipping delays across different shipping modes.
- Apply Pareto analysis to identify the sub-categories contributing to the majority of sales.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python**
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
- **Seaborn** – Statistical visualization

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading & Initial Exploration
- Importing the dataset using Pandas.
- Inspecting the dataset structure and initial records.
- Identifying missing values, empty strings, and zero values.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Converting Order Date and Ship Date into datetime format.
- Creating a new feature: `Shipping Delay`.
- Removing unnecessary columns.
- Handling missing values.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

**Sales Performance**
- Descriptive statistical analysis of sales.
- Total revenue calculation.
- Sales distribution and skewness analysis.

**Time-Based Analysis**
- Monthly sales trends.
- Yearly sales performance.
- Year-over-year sales comparison by category.
- Monthly sales heatmap.

**Product Analysis**
- Sales by category and sub-category.
- Top-performing products.
- Pareto analysis of sub-category sales.

**Customer Analysis**
- Identifying the top 10 customers by revenue.
- Comparing customer revenue and order counts.

**Geographical Analysis**
- Sales distribution across regions.
- Sales contribution by customer segment.
- Top 10 states based on total sales.

**Shipping Performance**
- Order volume and average sales by shipping mode.
- Average shipping delay by shipping mode.
- Shipping delay distribution and median comparison.
